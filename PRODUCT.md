# Product Specification: Rafito Juan Personal Portfolio

## Overview
Rafito Juan's personal developer portfolio showcasing technical experience, engineering projects, open-source work, academic achievements, client recommendations, and interactive mini-games.

## Target Audience
- Technical recruiters, engineering hiring managers, prospective clients, and developer community peers.

## Core Features & Sections
1. **Hero & Bio:** Quick introduction, pronunciation audio, role typewriter, location (Jakarta), contact links.
2. **About & GitHub Contributions:** Bio and real-time GitHub contribution graph visualization.
3. **Tech Stack:** Interactive categorizeable technology badges (Languages, Frameworks, Databases, Tools).
4. **Experience & Education:** Chronological employment journey, responsibilities, degree & academic highlights (SGPA 3.65 at Open University).
5. **Projects:** Curated list of fullstack, mobile, and AI engineering projects with live demos and repository links.
6. **Honors & Awards:** Accordion of competition wins, hackathons, and certifications with attached credentials.
7. **Client Testimonials:** Seamless continuous marquee with real-world recommendations from founders and peers.
8. **Interactive Games:** Embedded retro terminal games (Snake, Tetris, AI D&D text adventure).
9. **Contact:** Interactive messaging form with quick-template generators.
